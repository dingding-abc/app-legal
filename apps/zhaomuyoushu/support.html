Support

Last updated: November 28, 2026

1. Contact

For help with 朝暮有数, contact the independent developer at daniel.ding117@gmail.com. Last updated: November 28, 2026. Please include app version/build, iOS version, reproduction steps and redacted screenshots. Do not email payment card details, Apple credentials, raw purchase tokens or private ledgers.

2. AI Smart Bookkeeping subscription

Open Settings and tap the top dark AI Smart Bookkeeping banner to view monthly and annual plans, restore purchases or manage renewal. Basic manual bookkeeping, todos and multi-photo attachments remain free without a subscription.

3. AI voice, photo recognition and auto todos

Allow microphone, camera or photo access in iOS Settings, stay connected and check AI Pro status. Hold the record button to speak multiple expenses or phrases such as lending, borrowing, proxy buying or reimbursement; the app maps detected counterparties into Todo drafts alongside ledger items. For receipt photos, tap the AI Scan pill inside the note area. Always review drafts before confirming.

4. Multi-photo attachments and full editing

Both transactions and todos support taking multiple photos or selecting multiple images from Photos (up to nine compressed photos per item), grouped as thumbnails inside the note box. Tap any thumbnail to zoom smoothly or open native QuickLook preview, and use the delete icon or edit screen to add, replace or remove photos, records, categories, ledgers, custom currencies or RoundFace shortcuts. Toggle “Save Photos Inside App” in Settings → Preferences to keep independent compressed copies inside the app.

5. Backups, exports and TestFlight

Export CSV reports from Details, or manage manual iCloud snapshots when available. CSV files cannot restore the ledger. TestFlight purchases run in Apple’s sandbox environment without real charges and may use accelerated renewal periods; install the latest build when testing subscription authorization.

6. Cancellation and legal documents

Cancel renewal anytime through Apple Account subscription settings, including during a trial. Removing the app does not cancel an active subscription. For billing or refund requests, contact Apple Support. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
