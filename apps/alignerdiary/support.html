Support

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Need help with AlignerDiary?

Email the independent developer at daniel.ding117@gmail.com.

Report a problem

Include the app and iOS versions, device model, exact error, steps, expected result and what happened. Never send passwords; do not send a full backup or sensitive photos unless needed.

Purchases and access

Check your network and Apple account, then use Restore Purchases and retry verification without deleting records. The trial does not automatically charge you. See Terms or request a refund from Apple.

Backups and privacy

Export from Settings. Restore requires valid access and replaces current data. For iCloud, check your account, network and storage and keep the app in the foreground. Separate copies must be deleted separately. See Privacy.

Reminders and photos

Check system permissions, Focus and valid access. Follow your clinician's advice for treatment.
