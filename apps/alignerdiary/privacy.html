Aligner Tracker-AlignerDiary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

AlignerDiary is provided by an independent developer. Your diary stays on your device unless you choose to export or back it up.

For privacy questions or support, email daniel.ding117@gmail.com.

Information and use

Plans, wear logs, targets, feelings, notes, appointments, reminders, photos and preferences provide your records, statistics, comparisons and reminders. There is no developer account or diary upload to a developer backend, advertising, cross-app tracking or behavioral analytics.

Photos, permissions and widget

Camera and notification permissions are optional; existing photos are chosen with Apple's picker. The app keeps a processed copy without unnecessary EXIF, including location, and leaves Photos originals unchanged. Saving an independent JPEG to Photos requires add-only access; automatic saving is off by default. Saved images contain no notes or tray numbers, and imports are not automatically saved again. The widget uses limited wear, tray, appointment and access information, including check time and trial expiry, but no diary notes, photos, raw receipts or card details.

Optional backups

Exports contain your diary, photos and preferences; you choose the destination. There is no additional app password or encryption. Optional iCloud backup is off by default and stores private CloudKit snapshots in your Apple account; the developer cannot read them in the developer portal. It is not two-way sync and is attempted in the foreground without a guarantee. A local iCloud account identifier detects account changes. Account changes, turning backup off, restore, undoing a restore or clearing all local app data pause future uploads; an upload already underway may finish, and existing copies are not automatically removed. See Apple's privacy policy.

Restore replaces rather than merges current data. A recovery point is kept, but undoing restore discards later edits. Automatic saving to Photos is turned off after restore, undoing a restore or clearing all local app data.

Purchase verification

Apple StoreKit verifies trial and purchase transactions and original app information; the developer receives neither your diary for payment nor your card details. Time-verification data and the choice to dismiss the trial introduction are stored in Keychain and may remain after uninstall; they are not in diary backups. Clearing data does not reset a trial or revoke purchases.

Retention and choices

Local data remains until deleted, replaced or cleared. After trial expiry you can still view and export records, delete selected plans and clear all local data. Photos may remain in undo history, a recovery point or backups. Clearing all data removes the recovery point and resets settings and automatic Photos and cloud backup options. Local deletion does not delete independent Photos copies, exports, iCloud or device backups, Apple purchases or Keychain information. Manage each separately. You may decline or revoke permissions and disable optional features; the developer cannot remotely delete device or private cloud data.

Contact and updates

When you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments and use them to answer support or privacy requests. We retain correspondence as needed for the request and legal obligations; request deletion at the same address. Never send passwords. Share sensitive material only when necessary. Changes are reflected in the date above.
