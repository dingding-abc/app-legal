Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

AlignerDiary respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Data Collection

The developer does not operate a separate account system or developer-controlled backend for storing your personal app data. Information you create in the app is intended to remain under your control.

2. Local Storage and iCloud

App data may be stored locally on your device. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, data may also be synchronized through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services associated with your Apple account.

3. Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your payment card credentials.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The app does not use third-party advertising networks and is not intended to track you across apps or websites. If analytics or crash-reporting services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Control

You can manage information within the app. Locally stored information may also be removed by deleting the relevant data or uninstalling the app. iCloud-synchronized data is subject to the controls available through the app and your Apple account.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
