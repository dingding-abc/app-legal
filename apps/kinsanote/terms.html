Terms of Use

Last updated: November 28, 2026

These terms apply to Kinsanote, provided by an independent developer for organizing personal and family health records, checkup timelines and medical report images.

Archive purpose and free features

Kinsanote is designed solely for personal and family record keeping and checkup scheduling; it does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical interpretation or prescriptions, and should not replace professional healthcare judgment. Manual logging, multi-member notebooks, lossless multi-page report storage, pinch-to-zoom preview, redaction and full editing and deletion remain available without an AI subscription. Optional Family Lifetime unlocks expand local family archive capacity through a one-time purchase.

AI Pro subscriptions and trials

Kinsanote AI Pro is offered as Apple auto-renewable monthly, quarterly and annual subscriptions. The app displays local prices supplied by Apple, and the system purchase sheet determines the final currency and amount. Introductory free trials apply only when Apple confirms eligibility and automatically renew at the displayed plan price unless cancelled in time. AI can misread medical text, values or dates; always verify every AI draft against the original report before saving.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Apple charges your Apple Account and manages renewal. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends via Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal while access lasts through the active period reported by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Apple handles billing and refunds under applicable consumer rights.

Backups, availability and lawful use

AI features require connectivity and entitlement verification; service interruptions may prevent new AI requests while all local notebooks and report photos remain accessible. Maintain your own backups and ensure you have permission before uploading or sharing any family member’s report images. See the Privacy Policy for data handling details.

Updates and rights

Features and terms may change, with updates reflected in the app or the date above. These terms do not exclude non-waivable consumer rights. For help, visit the Support page or email the address above.
