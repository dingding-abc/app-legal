Kinsanote

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Kinsanote is provided by an independent developer. Your family member profiles, medical notebooks, checkup records and original report photos stay on your device unless you choose to sync them through your personal iCloud account or share a notebook via Apple Family Sharing.

For privacy questions or support, email daniel.ding117@gmail.com.

Medical records and lossless report photos

Family member profiles, medical notebooks, checkup records, follow-up reminders, preferences and multi-page report photos are stored locally in the app’s SQLite database and sandboxed document storage. No developer account is required, and there are no advertising or tracking analytics SDKs. Camera access is requested when scanning or taking photos, photo library access is requested when selecting or saving images, and notification access is requested when enabling checkup reminders. Deleting or replacing records or photos automatically cleans up unreferenced image files inside the app sandbox.

Optional AI report recognition

Manual record entry, multi-page report viewing, pinch-to-zoom inspection, redaction/blurring and full editing and deletion work without AI. Optional AI report recognition requires your explicit privacy consent (which you can grant or revoke anytime in Settings), network access and an active Kinsanote AI Pro subscription or eligible trial. When triggered, only the selected report images (which you may redact beforehand) together with your device’s local date/time and IANA time zone are sent over HTTPS to our Serverless AI gateway (Alibaba Cloud Function Compute with Qwen/DeepSeek in Mainland China, or Google Cloud Run with Gemini, OpenAI and DeepSeek fallback models overseas). No AI provider keys are stored in the app, and unrelated member profiles or other notebooks are never uploaded.

Processing and draft verification

The gateway verifies active entitlement before invoking AI models and does not maintain a persistent database of uploaded report photos or purchase credentials; inputs are processed solely to return structured extraction drafts. Cloud infrastructure may retain operational logs, and Apple, Alibaba Cloud and Google Cloud apply their own policies. Every AI result is presented as an editable draft candidate that must be reviewed against your original report before saving, and records clearly mark whether they were AI-extracted and verified or manually entered.

Optional iCloud and Apple Family Sharing

When enabled on iOS, optional iCloud storage uses Apple’s private CloudKit container associated with your Apple Account, not a developer-hosted medical database. Family Sharing permissions are controlled per medical notebook (Private vs. Family Shared). If sharing is turned off, a notebook is switched back to Private, or remote share access is revoked, the app immediately isolates and removes unauthorized shared notebook copies on the device.

Purchases and verification

Apple StoreKit processes optional one-time Family Lifetime unlocks and auto-renewable Kinsanote AI Pro subscriptions (monthly, quarterly and annual plans, with Family Sharing and eligible introductory free trials). The app reads verified transactions, local prices and entitlement status without receiving your payment card details.

Retention, choices and non-medical notice

Local data remains on your device until you edit or delete it. You can view, zoom, add, edit or delete any member, notebook, checkup record, reminder or individual report page inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes local app data subject to Apple device backup behavior and does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Kinsanote is a personal and family medical archive tool and does not provide clinical diagnosis, medical interpretation or treatment advice. Email correspondence is used only to respond to your request; changes are reflected in the date above.
