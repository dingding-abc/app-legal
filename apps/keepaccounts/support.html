朝暮有数

Support

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Contact

For help with 朝暮有数, contact the independent developer at daniel.ding117@gmail.com. Last updated: October 12, 2026. Include app version/build, iOS version, steps and a redacted screenshot. Do not send card details, Apple credentials, complete purchase tokens or a private ledger.

2. AI subscription

Open Settings → AI bookkeeping, directly above “Buy the author a coffee”. You can view Apple’s current monthly price, check trial eligibility, restore purchases and manage renewal. Apple decides seven-day trial eligibility. Basic bookkeeping and photo attachments remain free.

3. Voice requests and errors

Allow microphone access in iOS Settings, connect to the network, and check subscription status. Hold the recording control and review the resulting drafts before saving. If entitlement verification fails, retry or restore purchases using the same Apple Account. A restored purchase does not make an expired subscription active. AI results can contain errors.

4. TestFlight

TestFlight purchases use Apple’s testing environment and do not result in a real charge. Trial and renewal timing can be accelerated by Apple’s test settings; do not use sandbox timing to judge the production seven-day offer. Install a build that includes subscriptions; an older build cannot test the new gateway authorization.

5. Backups and photos

Use Settings → Data to create or restore a manual iCloud snapshot or export a CSV report. CSV is not a restorable backup. iCloud requires a signed-in Apple Account and available storage. Photo attachments stay in the ledger; saving a second copy to Photos is separately optional. Deleted local records may remain in older snapshots, exports or Photos.

6. Cancellation and documents

Use Apple subscription settings to cancel renewal, including during a trial. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. For purchase/refund issues, contact Apple through its purchase support. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
