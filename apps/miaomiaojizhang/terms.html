2. Free features and AI Pro

Manual bookkeeping, multi-ledger management, split bills, receivables/payables todos, multi-photo note attachments, native zoom preview, full editing and deletion remain free without an AI subscription. Only new AI voice bookkeeping and AI photo/receipt recognition require an active AI Pro subscription, eligible trial or Apple-confirmed grace period. AI can misunderstand speech, receipts, dates, amounts, categories or todo counterparties; review and edit every draft before saving.

3. Subscriptions and trials

AI Smart Bookkeeping (AI Pro) is offered as Apple auto-renewable monthly and annual subscriptions. The app displays local prices supplied by Apple, and the system purchase sheet determines the final currency and amount. Introductory free trials are offered only when Apple confirms eligibility. Confirming the offer starts a subscription that automatically renews at the displayed plan price unless cancelled in time.

4. Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Apple charges your Apple Account and manages renewal. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current subscription period ends to avoid the next renewal, via Settings → AI Smart Bookkeeping or Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal while access lasts through the active period reported by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Billing and refunds are governed by Apple and applicable consumer rights.

Any optional consumable tips offered in the app are strictly voluntary. They do not unlock AI Pro or other paid entitlements, are not subscriptions, and previously consumed tips cannot be restored as subscription access.
