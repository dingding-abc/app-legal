Terms of Use

Last updated: November 28, 2026

These terms apply to Swiftally, provided by an independent developer for personal and multi-ledger bookkeeping, split bills, receivables/payables tracking and optional iCloud family shared ledgers.

Questions: daniel.ding117@gmail.com.

Free features, AI Pro and Family Sharing Pack

Manual bookkeeping, multi-ledger management, split bills, receivables/payables todos, compressed multi-photo note attachments, native zoom preview, local snapshot export/restore, private iCloud snapshot backup, joining invited Family Shared Ledgers (adding, editing and deleting shared bills), and full local editing and deletion remain free without an AI subscription. Only new AI voice bookkeeping and AI photo/receipt recognition require an active AI Pro subscription, eligible trial or Apple-confirmed grace period. Creating and inviting others to an owned Family Shared Ledger requires either the one-time Family Sharing Pack (US$4.99 reference, Apple Family Sharing enabled), an active Monthly AI Pro subscription (valid while subscribed), or a paid Quarterly or Annual AI Pro subscription (permanently grants Family Sharing Pack access). AI can misunderstand speech, receipts, dates, amounts, categories or todo counterparties; review and edit every draft before saving. The app does not provide accounting, tax, investment or financial advice.

Subscriptions, Family Pack and trials

AI Smart Bookkeeping (AI Pro) is offered as Apple auto-renewable monthly, quarterly and annual subscriptions, and the Family Sharing Pack is offered as a one-time Non-Consumable purchase (and permanently unlocked for paid Quarterly/Annual AI Pro subscribers, or included while Monthly AI Pro is active). The app displays local prices supplied by Apple, and the system purchase sheet determines the final currency and amount. A 1-week free introductory trial for AI Pro is offered only when Apple confirms eligibility and automatically renews at the selected plan price unless cancelled in time.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Apple charges your Apple Account and manages renewal. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends via Settings → AI Smart Bookkeeping or Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal while access lasts through the active period reported by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to restore subscriptions and one-time Family Sharing Pack unlocks. Apple handles billing and refunds under applicable consumer rights.

Backups, availability and lawful use

AI features, iCloud private snapshots and Family Shared Ledgers require connectivity and valid Apple services; service interruptions may delay sync or prevent new AI requests while all local ledgers, todos and photo attachments remain accessible. Maintain your own backups and verify CSV or snapshot exports. Do not upload content you lack permission to use or use the service unlawfully. See the Privacy Policy for data handling and cloud providers.
