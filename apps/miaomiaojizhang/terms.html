Terms of Use

Last updated: November 28, 2026

These terms apply to Swiftally, provided by an independent developer for personal and multi-ledger bookkeeping, split bills and receivables/payables tracking.

Free features and AI Pro

Manual bookkeeping, multi-ledger management, split bills, receivables/payables todos, compressed multi-photo note attachments, native zoom preview and full editing and deletion remain free without an AI subscription. Only new AI voice bookkeeping and AI photo/receipt recognition require an active AI Pro subscription, eligible trial or Apple-confirmed grace period. AI can misunderstand speech, receipts, dates, amounts, categories or todo counterparties; review and edit every draft before saving. The app does not provide accounting, tax, investment or financial advice.

Subscriptions and trials

AI Smart Bookkeeping (AI Pro) is offered as Apple auto-renewable monthly and annual subscriptions. The app displays local prices supplied by Apple, and the system purchase sheet determines the final currency and amount. Introductory free trials are offered only when Apple confirms eligibility and automatically renew at the displayed plan price unless cancelled in time.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Apple charges your Apple Account and manages renewal. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends via Settings → AI Smart Bookkeeping or Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal while access lasts through the active period reported by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Any optional consumable tips do not unlock AI Pro and cannot be restored as subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refunds under applicable consumer rights.

Backups, availability and lawful use

AI features require connectivity and entitlement verification; service interruptions may prevent new AI requests while all local ledgers, todos and photo attachments remain accessible. Maintain your own backups and verify CSV exports. Do not upload content you lack permission to use or use the service unlawfully. See the Privacy Policy for data handling and cloud providers.

Updates and rights

Features and terms may change, with relevant updates reflected in the app or the date above. These terms do not exclude non-waivable consumer rights. For help, visit the Support page or email the address above.
