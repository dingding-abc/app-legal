Swiftally

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Swiftally is provided by an independent developer. Your ledgers, todos and compressed photo attachments stay on your device unless you choose to export or back them up.

For privacy questions or support, email daniel.ding117@gmail.com.

Ledger, todos and compressed photos

Ledgers, transactions, amounts, categories, split bills, receivables and payables todos, notes, custom currencies, shortcuts, preferences and multi-photo attachments (up to nine compressed JPEG photos per transaction or todo) are stored locally in the app’s SQLite database and app storage. No developer account is required, and there are no advertising or tracking analytics SDKs. Camera access is requested when taking photos, and photo library access is requested when choosing images. In Settings → Preferences, “Save Photos Inside App” is on by default to keep an independent compressed copy in the app sandbox so deleting originals from system Photos does not break viewing; turning it off references local URIs instead.

Optional AI voice and photo recognition

Manual bookkeeping, todos and photo attachments are free and work without AI. Optional AI voice bookkeeping (including automatic todo mapping for lend, borrow, proxy purchase and reimbursement phrases) and AI photo/receipt recognition require an active Apple AI Pro subscription or eligible trial, network access and microphone, camera or photo permission. When used, only the current voice recording or transcript, or the selected compressed receipt photo, plus active category names, ledger names, currency list, local date/time and IANA time zone are sent over HTTPS to our Serverless AI gateway (Alibaba Cloud Function Compute with Bailian/Qwen/DeepSeek in Mainland China, or Google Cloud Run with Gemini and fallback models overseas). No AI provider keys are stored in the app, and unrelated ledger history or other photos are never uploaded.

Processing and retention

The gateway verifies active entitlement before invoking AI models and does not create a persistent database of audio, transcripts, receipt images or purchase credentials; inputs are processed solely to return structured draft entries and todo suggestions. Temporary recordings on your device are deleted when the workflow finishes or is cancelled. Cloud infrastructure may retain operational logs, and Apple, Alibaba Cloud and Google Cloud apply their own retention policies. Every AI draft requires your review and confirmation before becoming a formal record and supports editing, undo and deletion.

iCloud, exports and exchange rates

Optional iCloud backup stores manual ledger snapshots, including photo attachments, in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. It is a manual snapshot rather than automatic multi-device synchronization. CSV reports are shared only through the destination you choose and cannot restore the ledger. Online exchange-rate updates send currency-code requests to public rate services and never transmit your ledger records.

Purchases and verification

Apple StoreKit processes AI Pro auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and annual plans) and any voluntary tips. The app reads verified transactions, local prices and entitlement status without receiving your payment card details. Voluntary tips are separate from AI access. iOS, TestFlight and hosting services may generate diagnostics under their own settings and policies.

Retention, choices and contact

Local data remains on your device until you edit or delete it. You can view, zoom, add, edit or delete any transaction, todo, photo attachment, category, ledger, custom currency or shortcut inside the app; deleting records or photos cleans up orphaned sandbox copies automatically, while copies in system Photos, shared CSV exports and older iCloud snapshots must be managed separately. Removing the app removes local app data subject to Apple device backup behavior and does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Email correspondence is used only to answer your request and retained as needed for support and legal obligations; changes are reflected in the date above.
